# Reference review and first finish option

Reviewed on 2026-09-05: [Walk through a Tropical-lux home in the east of Singapore](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EcJ51c-ZILI), i.Poise Design.
The page description identifies a condo at Meyer Road; it is an aesthetic
reference for this BTO, not evidence of its layout or dimensions.

Browser playback was accessible. Selected portions and paused frames were
reviewed, not the complete 12:14 tour. Local screenshots are retained in the
ignored `.cache/tropical-reference/` directory. Third-party video frames are
not shipped as textures or uploaded with the project.

| Viewed point | Visible observation | Adaptation in this release |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1:13 living overview | Warm brown timber cabinetry, pale floor, cognac seating, olive chair, cream cushions and greenery | Deeper timber and sand floor; retain coherent ivory sofa, use olive/cognac on existing accent textiles |
| 1:45 living joinery detail | Warm timber open shelving with concealed warm light | Use warmer timber finishes; retain calibrated lighting to avoid a yellow cast |
| 3:40 bedroom sample | Light bedding, brown upholstered headboards, framed dark botanical silhouettes and a pale woven-looking chair back | Ivory bedding and tan textiles; retain the project's original artwork and furniture geometry |
| 4:26 ceiling detail | Thin dark timber borders on pale ceiling | Material contrast informs the palette; no decorative ceiling geometry added |
| 6:28 bedroom detail | Warm strip-textured feature wall, ivory cabinetry and dark shelf accents | Coordinated sand cabinetry and darker restrained hardware; no wall remodel |

Exact species, leather/fabric composition, stone type and product models were
not established. “Teak”, “limestone” and “linen” in the palette describe proposed
appearances. Kitchen and bathroom reference coverage is incomplete, so their
finish changes extend the proposed palette rather than claim a room-for-room
reproduction. All three bedrooms, the current kitchen and both bathrooms remain.

The handover's full furniture expansion is broader than the immediate simple
switch. Version 0.11.0 provides two reversible finish sets on the same detailed
scene. It retains original minimalist datablocks, artwork, plants and geometry;
more extensive furnishing replacements can build on the explicit style IDs later.
